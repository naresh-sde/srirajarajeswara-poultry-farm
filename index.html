Our process, step by step

Nothing is re-packed twice. Here is exactly what happens between the hen and your tray.

01

Layer shed collection

Multiple early-morning collection rounds across 25,000 healthy layer birds, so no egg stays in the nest for long.

02

Grading & size sorting

Eggs are sorted by weight into uniform grades (about 52–58 g) so your customers get the same size every single time.

03

Candling lamp check

Every egg is held to a light to reject hairline cracks, blood spots and weak shells. Breakage stays under 1%.

04

UV sanitisation

Trays pass through a UV sanitising unit to reduce surface bacteria before packing into clean food-grade trays.

05

Dry, clean packing

Packed in fresh 30-egg trays and 180-egg cartons with a batch code for traceability of every delivery.

06

Cold store & dispatch

Held in a cool, dry store room and loaded onto our own vehicles — morning routes leave by 7:00 AM, next-morning orders by 6:00 PM.
